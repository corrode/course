Hi, I'm Matthias Endler, a Rust consultant and founder of corrode. I put this course together to help you get comfortable with Rust, one small program at a time. If you know variables, functions, and loops in another language, you're in the right place. You don't need any Rust experience.

Everything here is free, including the hints and solutions. Try the exercises right in your browser, with nothing to install. You can start without an account. Sign up whenever you'd like to save your progress by submitting solutions. Prefer your own editor? The README will help you get set up locally. Take your time, experiment, and don't be afraid to ask the compiler a few questions.

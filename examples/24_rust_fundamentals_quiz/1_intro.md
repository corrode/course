# Rust Fundamentals Quiz

*You can look things up here, just as you would while writing code.*

These questions revisit ownership and borrowing, `Option` and `Result`,
lifetimes, traits, enums, and modules, then ask you to predict behavior in the
CSV and environment-file projects. The `Rc` question includes the
shared-ownership rules you need; you don't need to have finished the
smart-pointer chapter. Pick an answer to lock it in and reveal the explanation
for every choice, not just the right one. The explanations for the other answers
are worth reading too, especially when you were choosing between two. Use Tab to
move between answers and Enter or Space to choose one. After answering every
question, you can reset the quiz and try again.

> [!TIP]
>
> You won't get a grade, there's no time limit, and the course doesn't keep a
> record of your answers.
